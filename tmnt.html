Get That Slice!

Presented by Ninja Turtles Pizza Company

Instructions

Objective:

The goal of the game is to collect as many pizza slices as possible while avoiding obstacles. As you collect pizza slices you’ll begin to level up and different collection items will start showing up … some easter eggs and special characters!

Controls:

  • Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to control the movement of your Ninja Turtle. W, A, S, D also work to move Leonardo.
  • Move up, down, left, and right to navigate through the pizza-filled maze.

Gameplay:

  • Your Ninja Turtle will move in the direction you press.
  • Collect as many pizza slices and other items as you can to earn points.
  • Beware of The Evil Shredder zooming by on his motorbike!
  • If you touch Shredder, you lose a life.
  • You have three lives, so use them wisely!
  • Your score is displayed at the top of the game screen.

Special Power-ups:

  • Look out for power-up items that may appear throughout the game.
  • Power-ups provide you with extra lives.
  • These power ups come in the form of a heart – collect them to keep going!

Winning the Game:

  • The game continues until you lose all your lives.
  • Challenge yourself to get the highest score and become a pizza-eating champion!

Tips:

  • Plan your moves to avoid enemies and grab pizza efficiently.
  • Watch enemy patterns and adjust your strategy.
  • Use power-ups strategically to maximize your score.
