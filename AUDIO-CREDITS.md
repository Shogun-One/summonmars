# Ocean of Aliens — audio sources

The following combat-vocal source recordings are distributed under CC0 or an
equivalent public-domain dedication. They were edited, trimmed, pitch-shifted,
normalized, and/or given echo for use in the game.

- `pilot-male-hit.mp3` — derived from **Male pain grunts** by stilgar (public domain): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Male_pain_grunts.ogg
- `pilot-male-defeat-short.mp3` and `pilot-male-defeat.mp3` — derived from **ahhh** by UncleSigmund (CC0): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UncleSigmund_-_ahhh_(cc0)_(freesound).mp3
- `pilot-female-hit.mp3`, `pilot-female-defeat-short.mp3`, and `pilot-female-defeat.mp3` — derived from **Demonic Woman Scream** by nick121087 (CC0): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nick121087_-_Demonic_Woman_Scream_(cc0)_(freesound).mp3
- `boss-defeat.mp3` — derived from **Monster growls** by stilgar (public domain): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Monster_growls.ogg
