How to Play

Everything an explorer needs to trace words, master boards, manage rewards, and build a complete Verbia loadout.

  1. ☝
    Trace and submit words

    Touch a letter, glide through other letters, and release to submit. While tracing, slide back over the previous letter to remove it. Alerts identify accepted, repeated, and unrecognized words.

  2. ✦
    Stage words and bonus words

    Stage words advance the board and award 10 Score plus 10 Discovery Points. Other valid dictionary words become Bonus Discoveries and award 5 Score plus 5 Discovery Points.

  3. ✓
    Clear or master a board

    Find the required number of stage words to clear the board and activate the pulsing green arrow. Find every stage word to master it; the arrow then pulses and shakes, the letters lock, and the board waits for you to continue.

  4. ◈
    Score versus Discovery Points

    Score records your word-play achievement and never drops during a game. Discovery Points are your spendable balance: rewards add to them and store purchases subtract from them. Both reset only when you confirm New Game.

  5. ↻
    Shuffle, continue, and revisit

    Shuffle rearranges an active constellation without changing its letters. The green right arrow advances after clearing; the left arrow can revisit up to five recently cleared boards that still have words to discover.

  6. 💡
    Hints

    Three hints refresh daily. Tap the lightbulb to reveal help; its message remains open until you close it. Hint packs can be purchased repeatedly, and newly added hints make the lightbulb pulse until used.

  7. 🎯
    Darts and Daily Orbit

    Your daily dart and any purchased or discovered darts appear on the board. Tap the pulsing target to throw at Daily Orbit for hints or Discovery Points. Newly received darts pulse until the target is opened.

  8. 📖
    Explorer Dictionary

    Purchase the SCOWL Dictionary once to place its book on the game board and showroom floor. Open it for the A–Z wheel, choose one of five sections for each letter, then scroll the readable word list.

  9. ⛏
    Treasure Cave

    Each expedition costs 100 Discovery Points, with three available per day. Choose one of four caves for a weighted surprise: minerals sold for points, rare high-value relics, hint caches, or reward darts. Possible prize types and values appear below the caves.

  10. 🛍
    Explorer Store

    Use Discovery Points to unlock explorers, companions, ships, vehicles, and helpers. The hanging paper shows your live balance. Selecting or buying a loadout item keeps you in the store and returns you to the fitting bay to see it.

  11. 🎒
    Inventory Showroom

    Choose any owned explorer, companion, and vehicle, or tap an equipped companion or vehicle to remove it. The fitting-bay sign shows permanent Score. Store and inventory icons on the board provide quick access.

  12. ⚙
    Pause menu and New Game

    Pause to resume, review this guide, choose slower or faster music, change sound, haptics, cosmic theme, or letter shape, and view your wallet. New Game permanently resets progress, Score, Discovery Points, purchases, and daily cave use; the two robot explorers remain free.
