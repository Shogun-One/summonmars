How to Play

Trace the constellation, discover real words, and build your explorer’s world.

  1. ☝
    Trace words

    Touch a letter, glide through adjacent choices, and release to submit. Slide back over the previous letter to undo it.

  2. 60%
    Clear each board

    Reach the glowing mark on the progress bar to unlock the next board. Find every stage word to master it.

  3. ★
    Find bonus words

    Valid words outside the stage list earn extra points and fly into Bonus Discoveries.

  4. 💡
    Hints and daily rewards

    Three hints refresh daily. The Daily Orbit dart board can award more hints or discovery points.

  5. ↻
    Shuffle and revisit

    Shuffle letters for a fresh view, or use the left arrow to revisit up to five incomplete cleared boards.

  6. 🛍
    Customize your explorer

    Spend discovery points in the Explorer Shop, then equip realistic clothing, accessories, and pets in the Inventory Showroom.
